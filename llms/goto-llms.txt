# GoTo

> GoTo (PT GoTo Gojek Tokopedia Tbk, IDX: GOTO) is the largest technology group in Indonesia, formed in 2021 from the merger of Gojek and Tokopedia. It operates a super-app ecosystem across on-demand services, e-commerce, and financial technology (GoTo Financial / GoPay), serving Indonesia's 270M+ population. Developer surfaces are published per product rather than at a single group-level developer portal; this profile covers the parent holding company.
